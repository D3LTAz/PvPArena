## 2. Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

**Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement**

This Agreement is entered into as of [DATE] between **[STUDIO LEGAL NAME]** ("Company") and **[RECIPIENT NAME]** ("Recipient"), in connection with Recipient's access to pre-release information about PvP Arena (the "Purpose").

### 2.1 Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either Party, whether written, oral, or visual, including but not limited to: game design documents, source code, build files, art assets, gameplay footage, marketing plans, business/financial information, and details of unreleased features — including, specifically, any pre-release information about token purchases, the virtual economy, or PvP wagering mechanics. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Recipient; (b) was already known to Recipient without an obligation of confidentiality; (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice to the disclosing Party where legally permitted).

### 2.2 Obligations
Recipient agrees to: (a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without Company's prior written consent; (c) protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care Recipient uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; and (d) limit access to Confidential Information to those who need it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.

### 2.3 No License; No Public Disclosure
Nothing in this Agreement grants Recipient any license or rights in Company's intellectual property beyond what is necessary to review the Confidential Information for the Purpose. Recipient will not publish, stream, screenshot, or otherwise publicly disclose any pre-release material (including gameplay footage, art, or written details) without Company's prior written consent — this includes social media, streaming platforms, and press.

### 2.4 Term
This Agreement's confidentiality obligations begin on the Effective Date and continue for [X years — TBD, e.g., 3-5 years, or "until the information becomes public through Company's own release," TBD by counsel] after the date of disclosure, regardless of when the Purpose ends.

### 2.5 Return or Destruction of Materials
Upon Company's request or termination of the Purpose, Recipient will promptly return or destroy all materials containing Confidential Information and confirm destruction in writing, except as required to be retained by law or standard backup procedures (which remain subject to this Agreement's confidentiality terms).

### 2.6 Remedies
Recipient acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages may be inadequate, and Company may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies available at law.

### 2.7 General
This Agreement does not create any obligation to enter into a business relationship. Governing law: [JURISDICTION — TBD]. Entire agreement; amendments must be in writing.
